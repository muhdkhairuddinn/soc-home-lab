# Part 3: Investigation write-ups

**Coming soon.** Nothing is written here yet, and I'd rather say that than fake it.

The plan: take a real alert from the lab and answer, in plain English:

1. What happened?
2. Was it normal, or weird?
3. How do I know?
4. What would I change so it's less noisy next time?

First two cases I want to do:

- **A failed Windows login, then a successful one.** One honest typo on my own VM's lock
  screen. (Spoiler: it's a typo, not a hacker.)
- **A failed security setting** from Wazuh's built-in configuration scan. Not an attack,
  just "this setting could be safer".
