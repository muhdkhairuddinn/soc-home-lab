# Day 28: Detection coverage map

**Goal:** See which attacker techniques your lab can and cannot detect.

## Read (15 minutes)

- [MITRE ATT&CK Navigator](https://mitre-attack.github.io/attack-navigator/)

## Do

1. Open the ATT&CK Navigator and create a new layer.
2. Colour the techniques your rules and the built-in rules cover (from Day 12).
3. Save the layer JSON and take a screenshot.

## Check yourself

- Where is the biggest gap?
- Which gap could you close with one new rule?
