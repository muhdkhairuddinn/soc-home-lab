# Day 25: Export and report

**Goal:** Share findings without leaking anything private.

## Read (15 minutes)

- [Wazuh dashboard](https://documentation.wazuh.com/current/user-manual/wazuh-dashboard/index.html)

## Do

1. Use **Reporting** in the dashboard to export a CSV or PDF of one search.
2. Open it and check for anything private: usernames, hostnames, IPs, passwords.
3. Decide what you can publish. In this lab everything is fake, but build the habit now.

## Check yourself

- What would you remove before sending this to a manager?
