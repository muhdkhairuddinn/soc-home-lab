# Day 22: Triage method

**Goal:** Handle any alert the same way every time.

## Do

Use the four questions from [Part 3](../03-investigations.md) as your template:

1. What happened?
2. Was it normal or weird?
3. How do I know?
4. What would I change?

Pick three alerts from your dashboard and answer them in 5 lines each. Aim for under 10 minutes per alert.

## Check yourself

- Which alert took longest? What information was missing?
